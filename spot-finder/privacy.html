Spot Finder – Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Spot Finder is a simple offline GPS tool for saving named locations ("spots") and navigating back to them. This page explains what data the app uses and how it is handled.

What we use

Spot Finder uses your phone's GPS (precise location) and compass so you can save named spots and navigate back to them with an on-screen arrow and proximity beeps.

What stays on your phone

Saved coordinates and names are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Spot Finder does not require an account, and does not upload, sell, or share your location with anyone. The app works fully offline and does not use any maps SDK or analytics/advertising service.

No cloud backup of spots

Android Auto Backup is disabled for this app, so your saved coordinates are not copied to Google Drive or any other cloud storage by the system.

Permissions

Location is requested only while you use the app. Precise (fine) location is required — approximate location is not accurate enough for finding a spot a few meters away. You can revoke this permission at any time in your device's system settings.

Children

Spot Finder is a general-purpose navigation aid. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date.
