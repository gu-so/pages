Hytteyatzy – Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Hytteyatzy is a Scandinavian Yatzy dice game for one or more players on the same phone, or at a shared table over Bluetooth. This page explains what data the app uses and how it is handled.

What we use

Hytteyatzy uses your device’s sensors and Bluetooth only as needed to play:

  • Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) — optional “Host table / Join table” multiplayer so nearby phones can share a game. Player names and game moves are exchanged only between devices in the room over the local BLE link. They are not sent to our servers (there are none).
  • Accelerometer — optional firm shake-to-roll on your turn.
  • Location (Android 11 and older only) — on some older Android versions, scanning for Bluetooth devices requires the location permission. Hytteyatzy does not use GPS coordinates and does not track where you are. On Android 12+, BLE is requested with “never for location” where the platform supports it.
  • Vibration — light feedback for some actions, if the device allows it.

What stays on your phone

The app may store a display name and a short list of past game summaries (winners and scores) in the app’s private storage so you do not have to retype your name and can recall recent cabin sessions. This data does not leave your device. Hytteyatzy does not require an account, and does not use analytics, advertising, or cloud game servers.

No cloud backup of game data

Android Auto Backup is disabled for this app, so your local name and game history are not copied to Google Drive or other cloud backup by the system.

Permissions

Bluetooth permissions are requested only when you choose Host or Join table. You can revoke permissions at any time in your device’s system settings. Single-phone (solo / pass-and-play) modes work without Bluetooth.

Children

Hytteyatzy is a general-purpose family dice game. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “last updated” date.
