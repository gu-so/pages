dB Meter – Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

dB Meter estimates sound level using your phone’s microphone. This page explains what data the app uses and how it is handled.

What we use

dB Meter uses the microphone only while measurement is running, so it can compute an approximate level on the device. Optional calibration trim is stored on the phone.

What stays on your phone

dB Meter does not require an account. It does not upload audio or measurements to a developer cloud service, and it does not use analytics or advertising SDKs.

Permissions

The app requests microphone access for measurement. You can deny the permission, force-stop, or uninstall the app at any time.

Accuracy

Phone microphones are not certified sound level meters. Readings are approximate and can vary by device, case, and processing. The app is not intended for legal, medical, or workplace compliance measurements.

Children

dB Meter is a general-purpose utility. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “last updated” date.
