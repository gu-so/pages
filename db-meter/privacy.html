dB Meter – Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

dB Meter estimates ambient sound level from the microphone. Readings are uncalibrated estimates, not lab-grade measurements.

What we use

The microphone is used while the meter is active to sample audio amplitude on the device. Audio is not recorded to a file for upload, and is not sent to any server.

What stays on your phone

dB Meter does not require an account. No analytics or advertising SDKs are included. Mic samples are processed locally for the live level display.

Permissions

Microphone permission is required for the meter to work. You can revoke it in system settings at any time.

Children

dB Meter is a general-purpose utility. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “last updated” date.
