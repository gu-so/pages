Unit Converter – Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Unit Converter converts common units offline on your device.

What we use

The app only uses the values you type to compute conversions. It does not need location, camera, microphone, or Bluetooth.

What stays on your phone

Unit Converter does not require an account and does not send your inputs to any server. No analytics or advertising SDKs are included.

Permissions

No special dangerous permissions are required.

Children

Unit Converter is a general-purpose utility. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “last updated” date.
