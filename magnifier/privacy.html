Magnifier – Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Magnifier uses the camera for a zoomed live view, with optional freeze and torch.

What we use

The camera (and optional LED torch) run while you use the magnifier. Frames are shown on screen for magnification. The app is not a photo-sharing or cloud camera service.

What stays on your phone

Magnifier does not require an account. Camera frames are processed on the device for display and are not uploaded. No analytics or advertising SDKs are included.

Permissions

Camera permission is required. You can revoke it in system settings.

Children

Magnifier is a general-purpose utility. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “last updated” date.
