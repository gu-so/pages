DogCam – Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

DogCam lets one Android phone act as a camera and another as a viewer over your own network (for example LAN or Tailscale). This page explains what data the app uses and how it is handled.

What we use

On the camera phone, DogCam uses the camera and microphone to encode a local video/audio stream and optional motion or loud-sound alerts. On the viewer phone, DogCam connects to the camera phone’s address that you scan or enter, and may use the microphone for optional talkback.

What stays on your devices

DogCam does not require an account with us. It does not use analytics or advertising SDKs. Pairing details (stream URL / secret) are stored on the phone so you can resume after restart. Video and audio travel only between the phones you configure (your LAN or your own mesh such as Tailscale). We do not operate a cloud backend for DogCam streams.

Permissions

Typical permissions include camera, microphone, and notifications (so the stream or standby watch can keep running). Network access is used only to reach the other phone you pair with. You can stop the broadcast, stop watching, or uninstall the app at any time.

Children

DogCam is a general-purpose utility. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “last updated” date.
