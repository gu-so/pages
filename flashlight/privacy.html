Flashlight – Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Flashlight controls the device LED torch and/or a full-screen light (including night-red), with optional strobe.

What we use

Camera / flash hardware may be used to turn the LED torch on and off. No photos or video are captured for storage or upload when using the torch.

What stays on your phone

Flashlight does not require an account and does not include analytics or advertising SDKs.

Permissions

Camera permission may be required by Android to control the flash/torch. You can revoke it in system settings (the LED torch feature may stop working).

Children

Flashlight is a general-purpose utility. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “last updated” date.
