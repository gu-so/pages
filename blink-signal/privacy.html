Blink Signal – Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Blink Signal is an offline optical chat tool: one phone transmits short codes with the camera flash; another receives with the camera. No internet is required for the core loop.

What we use

Camera — for receiving light patterns (and related processing on device).
Camera flash / torch — for transmitting light patterns.

Message content you choose to send is encoded into light on the device. The app does not upload messages or camera frames to any server.

What stays on your phone

Blink Signal does not require an account. There is no cloud messaging backend and no analytics or advertising SDK. Any local UI state stays on the device.

Permissions

Camera permission is required for transmit/receive. You can revoke it in system settings.

Children

Blink Signal is a general-purpose utility. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “last updated” date.
