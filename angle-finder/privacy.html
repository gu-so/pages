Angle Finder – Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Angle Finder is a digital protractor that measures the angle between two orientations of your phone using motion sensors.

What we use

The app uses the accelerometer / related sensors while you capture two readings and displays the angle between them.

What stays on your phone

Angle Finder does not require an account and does not upload measurements. No analytics or advertising SDKs are included.

Permissions

No location, microphone, or camera permission is required.

Children

Angle Finder is a general-purpose utility. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “last updated” date.
