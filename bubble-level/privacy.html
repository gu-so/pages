Bubble Level – Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Bubble Level is a two-axis spirit level using your phone’s motion sensors.

What we use

The app reads the accelerometer (and related motion sensors) while open so it can show whether a surface is level, with optional sound when level.

What stays on your phone

Bubble Level does not require an account and does not send sensor data to the internet. There is no analytics or advertising SDK.

Permissions

No location, microphone, or camera permission is required.

Children

Bubble Level is a general-purpose utility. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “last updated” date.
