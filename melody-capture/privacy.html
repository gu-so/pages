Melody Capture – Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Melody Capture turns monophonic sound from your phone’s microphone into notes and a simple staff. This page explains what data the app uses and how it is handled.

What we use

Melody Capture uses the microphone only while you are capturing, so it can estimate pitch and note timing on the device. Optional export (for example MIDI) is saved or shared by you using the system share sheet.

What stays on your phone

Melody Capture does not require an account. It does not upload audio or notes to a developer cloud service, and it does not use analytics or advertising SDKs. Processing happens on the device. Any files you export are under your control.

Permissions

The app requests microphone access for capture. You can deny the permission, force-stop, or uninstall the app at any time.

Children

Melody Capture is a general-purpose utility. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “last updated” date.
