Speedometer – Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Speedometer shows approximate speed from GPS (knots, km/h, or mph).

What we use

The app uses your phone’s location services (GPS / network location) while open so it can display speed. Coordinates are used only to derive speed and related readout; the app is not a trip logger.

What stays on your phone

Speedometer does not require an account. It does not upload your location or speed to any server, and it does not include analytics or advertising SDKs. Readings are processed on the device.

Permissions

Location is requested so the app can obtain GPS speed. You can revoke location access in system settings; the speed readout will not work without it.

Children

Speedometer is a general-purpose utility. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “last updated” date.
