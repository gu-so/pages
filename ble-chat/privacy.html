BLE Chat – Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

BLE Chat is a nearby encrypted chat over Bluetooth Low Energy (1:1 and small group rooms). Messages travel between phones in range over BLE, not through a developer cloud service.

What we use

  • Bluetooth Low Energy — to discover, connect, and exchange encrypted chat messages with nearby devices you choose to pair with.
  • Location (where the platform requires it for BLE scan) — on some Android versions, scanning for Bluetooth devices requires location permission. BLE Chat does not use GPS to track your geographic location as a feature; location is requested only because of platform rules for BLE scanning on those versions.

Display names and chat content you enter are handled on your device and sent only over the local BLE link to peers in the chat. End-to-end encryption is used so that other BLE devices that are not part of your chat cannot read message payloads in the intended design.

What stays on your phone

BLE Chat may keep a local display name and optional local message history on the device (you can use ephemeral mode where available so history is not kept). There is no developer-operated message server, and no analytics or advertising SDK. Android Auto Backup is disabled so chat data is not copied to cloud backup by the system.

Permissions

Bluetooth (and on some devices location-for-scan) permissions are requested so nearby chat can work. You can revoke permissions in system settings; multiplayer chat will not work without Bluetooth access.

Children

BLE Chat is a general-purpose utility. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “last updated” date.
